# Application Lenses — Non-Load-Bearing

These examples helped reveal the general architecture. They are illustrative, not evidence for MAGIKARP v0.1.

## 1. Seven deadly sins as authority-capture lens

A functional reinterpretation explored the classical vices as recurrent control failures rather than literal computational equivalents.

Candidate mappings:

- Pride — self-certainty outruns corrective evidence.
- Envy — comparative status substitutes for independent valuation.
- Greed — acquisition objective weakens stopping conditions.
- Gluttony — consumption outruns marginal functional value.
- Lust — immediate salience dominates longer-horizon constraints.
- Wrath — affective intensity captures policy before adequate deliberation.
- Sloth — effort avoidance prevents accepted correction from reaching execution.

The useful abstraction was not “sins = CARP opposites.”

It was:

> A locally compelling signal can acquire authority outside its warranted domain, scope, magnitude, or duration.

The corresponding virtues were reinterpreted as possible regulatory dispositions preserving correction capacity.

Important scope limit: this is a modern functional reinterpretation, not a historical claim that Christian moral taxonomy was designed as control theory.

## 2. Music taxonomy as representational-governance lens

A long-running personalized music taxonomy provided a low-stakes example where the authority criterion is not scientific truth.

Candidate categories included:

- OVERDRIVE — energy becomes physical pressure/impact.
- FESTIVAL — individual energy becomes collective synchronization.
- VELOCITY — motion itself is rewarding.
- ASCENSION — emotional transformation.
- DREAMSTATE — remaining inside a compelling state.
- EXPANSION — the perceived world enlarges/unfolds.

The useful claim is not that these are objective natural kinds.

A narrower claim is:

> The taxonomy may be a stable compression of recurring listener-level distinctions if it predicts held-out judgments and survives correction without arbitrary category proliferation.

Two symmetric failures:

### Frozen representation
Every novel track is forced into existing categories.

### Fragmented representation
Every anomaly creates a new category.

The target is:

> Preserve useful distinctions while allowing representational revision when predictive compression genuinely fails.

This example helped generalize the umbrella from scientific truth correction to representational governance.

## 3. Why these examples remain archived

They are useful for:

- intuition,
- teaching,
- stress-testing portability,
- generating future toy tasks.

They should not be used as evidence that SD predicts adaptation in the v0.1 scientific sense.
