# Informal External Assessment Notes

This file records an informal assessment supplied during development. It is not independent scientific validation and should not be cited as evidence for MAGIKARP's empirical claims.

## Summary of the supplied Grok assessment

The assessment characterized the broader lineage as unusually coherent and self-constraining, emphasizing:

- Negative-Space Search as a scaffold for governing exploration of missing structure.
- Correctable Lineage as a complementary scaffold for governing inheritance of validated structure.
- The pairing as the stronger object, especially the separation:
  - discovery ≠ validation,
  - attention allocation ≠ authority allocation.
- Empirical claim contraction under hostile benchmarks as a methodological positive.
- The Controlled Adaptation Reasoning Protocol as a compact runtime expression of the same discipline.
- Open questions around comparative performance, independent reconstruction, and transfer beyond synthetic settings.

The assessment gave high conceptual/architectural marks but lower scientific-maturity marks, correctly identifying external comparative tests as the bottleneck.

## Useful takeaway

The most valuable part of the assessment was not the rating.

It reinforced the cross-lineage invariant:

\[
\text{possibility generation} \neq \text{authority assignment}
\]

and the methodological principle that conceptual coherence should not substitute for external testing.

## Current interpretation after MAGIKARP contraction

The lineage is now best read as:

- Negative-Space Search — preserve useful possibility.
- Correctable Lineage — preserve justified inheritance.
- CARP — candidate operational discipline.
- Representational Governance — umbrella problem.
- MAGIKARP v0.1 — narrow empirical test of whether representation self-diagnosis predicts later adaptation beyond existing measures.

The informal assessment remains context, not authority.
