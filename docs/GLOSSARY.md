# Glossary

## Representational Governance
Umbrella problem: how representations acquire, retain, relinquish, and revise authority under changing conditions.

## MAGIKARP
Research program/codename for studying preserved justified transformation, currently narrowed in v0.1 to the predictive value of representation self-diagnosis.

## CARP
Controlled Adaptation Reasoning Protocol. A candidate operational method, not the definition of MAGIKARP success.

## Q
Present competence before the relevant perturbation.

## SD
Representation self-diagnosis measured before unrestricted adaptation. In v0.1, primarily the ability to discriminate parameter, model, and interface failure.

## F
Experimenter-controlled ground-truth failure depth.

## F_p
Parameter failure: local parameter/value is wrong while current representation/model class remains adequate.

## F_m
Model failure: represented variables are adequate but relationships/mechanism among them are wrong.

## F_i
Interface failure: the representation/observation interface collapses a distinction needed for correct behavior.

## Y_adapt
Held-out adaptation trajectory, kept as a vector rather than a universal scalar.

## R_c
Recovery after adaptation.

## T
Transfer of the correction to unseen related conditions.

## R
Retention of the correction over time/intervening tasks.

## P
Preservation of unaffected valid structure.

## C
Correction/revision cost, especially unnecessary change.

## E
Ordinary/raw error sensitivity used as a control.

## A
Standard adaptation-relevant baseline measures used as controls.

## JT
Justified transformability: capacity to change when warranted, resist unwarranted change, and preserve unaffected structure. Explanatory reserve in v0.1.

## Escape topology
The set/structure of effective pathways by which a system can leave a state established as defective. Explanatory reserve in v0.1.

## Transformation topology
The broader structure of justified future transformations reachable from the current state. Explanatory reserve in v0.1.

## Authority capture
Self-reinforcing authority misallocation that weakens mechanisms capable of correcting it. Explanatory reserve in v0.1.

## Gyarados failure
Secondary hypothesis where current representation/task performance improves while self-diagnosis or justified transformability declines.

## Adequacy sensitivity
The capacity for failures of a representation to become evidence about the limits of that representation.

## Failure independence
The property that correction mechanisms do not all collapse under the same relevant latent failure.

## Temporal firewall
Requirement that SD be measured before later adaptation outcomes.

## Data firewall
Requirement that diagnostic and adaptation phases be separated enough to prevent benchmark recognition or information leakage.
