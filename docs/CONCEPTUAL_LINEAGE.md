# Conceptual Lineage

This is a research-development history, not a claim that every stage is empirically established.

## 1. Negative-Space Search

Early question:

> What possibilities or distinctions is the current search/representation space failing to expose?

Core constraint:

\[
\text{possibility generation} \neq \text{authority assignment}
\]

Search may allocate attention to candidate missing structure, but absence is not automatic evidence of opportunity and speculation does not earn truth authority by being generated.

The empirical program reportedly contracted toward a narrower result: boundary-gated search may improve representational/search economy rather than invention quality itself.

## 2. Correctable Lineage

Complementary question:

> Which validated structures deserve to persist, with what provenance, scope, and reopening conditions?

Core separation:

\[
\text{discovery} \neq \text{validation}
\]

and:

\[
\text{attention allocation} \neq \text{authority allocation}
\]

The pairing with Negative-Space Search suggested two coupled control problems:

- preserve useful possibility;
- preserve justified inheritance.

## 3. Interface diagnosis and correction ecosystems

The work then separated several failure levels:

- parameter/utilization failure,
- model/mechanism failure,
- interface/non-identifiability failure.

A key recurring condition:

\[
O(s_a)=O(s_b)
\quad\text{while}\quad
L(s_a)\neq L(s_b)
\]

No optimizer over the collapsed interface can solve the distinction without additional information.

A further result concerned common-mode correction failure: multiple correction channels can appear diverse while sharing an upstream dependency.

This motivated the distinction:

\[
\text{plurality} \neq \text{failure independence}
\]

## 4. CARP

The conceptual work compressed into a practical reasoning protocol:

- localize,
- separate possibility from authority,
- scope claims,
- discriminate efficiently,
- revise minimally,
- retest,
- separate belief from action under urgency.

CARP became an operational candidate, not a complete theory.

## 5. Authority capture and correction topology

A later theoretical exploration described recurrent failures as authority misallocation and self-reinforcing capture.

Important distinctions included:

- misallocation — state property,
- capture — dynamical property,
- governance/meta-capture — revision/adjudication pathways become compromised,
- interface capture — relevant distinctions cease to become representable.

The correction-path idea evolved from path count to dependency topology:

\[
\text{redundancy} \neq \text{diversity}
\]

Nominally separate channels that collapse under the same latent failure offer little true redundancy.

These ideas are retained as explanatory reserve, not as v0.1 requirements.

## 6. MAGIKARP

The name became useful once the core distinction was framed as:

\[
\text{state quality} \neq \text{transition quality}
\]

The research question shifted from:

> How should a system correct errors?

to:

> What properties determine whether a system can still become appropriately different when its current representation becomes inadequate?

This yielded the notion of justified transformability and the secondary “Gyarados failure”:

\[
Q_{\text{state}}\uparrow,\quad JT\downarrow
\]

or more sharply:

\[
Q(R)\uparrow,\quad SD(R)\downarrow
\]

## 7. Representational Governance

The umbrella concept emerged when the framework was generalized beyond scientific truth claims.

Representations in different domains can earn authority under different warrant functions, while sharing a governance question:

> How do representations acquire, retain, relinquish, and revise authority?

This prevented a category error: a representation can fail structurally without being a false scientific proposition.

## 8. Empirical contraction to v0.1

The project then deliberately reduced its ontology.

The empirical hinge is now:

\[
SD(R)\rightarrow Y_{\text{adapt}}
\]

with prediction tested before causation.

Frozen claim:

> Does pre-adaptation representation self-diagnosis add held-out predictive information about later adaptation beyond current competence, raw error sensitivity, and standard adaptation measures?

That is the first result the project now needs.
