# Conceptual Reserve

These concepts were useful in reaching the v0.1 hypothesis but are **not load-bearing requirements** for the first benchmark.

They should be activated only when empirical results require them.

## Justified transformability (JT)

Not maximal plasticity.

\[
JT \approx \text{capacity to make warranted changes, resist unwarranted ones, and preserve unaffected structure}
\]

Two symmetric failures:

- rigidity — warranted change does not occur;
- hyperplasticity — unwarranted challenge causes unnecessary restructuring.

## Escape topology

A correction-focused subset of transformation topology.

Question:

> Can the system get out of a state established as defective?

Distinguish:

\[
\mathcal P^{latent}
\supseteq
\mathcal P^{accessible}
\supseteq
\mathcal P^{effective}
\]

A path can exist, be reachable, and still lack causal authority to change the system.

## Transformation topology

The broader set of warranted future transformations still reachable from the present state.

The research program originally emphasized:

\[
\text{current capability} \neq \text{preserved transformation capacity}
\]

This remains useful but should not be reconstructed in full for v0.1.

## Authority allocation

Signals may legitimately govern different domains, scopes, magnitudes, and durations.

The general failure is persistent authority misallocation, not simply “authority other than evidence.”

Evidence governs epistemic authority; action also depends on goals, values, costs, and constraints.

## Authority capture

A self-reinforcing misallocation in which a subsystem gains unwarranted control and degrades mechanisms that could reduce that control.

## Governance/meta-capture

The captured process begins controlling adjudication or revision mechanisms.

Key idea:

\[
\text{authority controls what happens}
\]

versus:

\[
\text{authority controls how its own authority is revised}
\]

The latter is structurally more dangerous.

## Interface capture

The process controls which distinctions can even become visible as candidates for correction.

A possible feedback loop:

\[
\text{capture}
\rightarrow
\text{reduced observability}
\rightarrow
\text{apparent confirmation}
\rightarrow
\text{stronger capture}
\]

This can turn absence of correction signals into apparent validation.

## Failure independence

The relevant unit of redundancy is not mechanism count but failure-path diversity.

\[
\text{independence} \neq \text{separation}
\]

Two physically separate critics may be epistemically equivalent if they share the same latent blind spot, observation channel, incentive, or preprocessing defect.

Diagnostic question:

> If this authority is wrong, what else must be wrong for it to remain uncorrected?

## Correction cut sets

A possible future formalization asks for the minimal set of causally distinct failures required to disable all effective correction paths.

This could yield a robustness quantity, but v0.1 does not require it.

## Provenance preservation

Authority is not fully characterized by current confidence.

Earlier work tracked dimensions such as validity, provenance, mechanism, future reliability, and reopening conditions.

A later authority-history representation was sketched as:

\[
A_t=(V,P,M,S,R,H)
\]

The concern is provenance laundering: adaptively acquired authority is retrospectively reclassified as foundational to insulate it from challenge.

Useful invariant:

> Authority may change class; its provenance should not be retroactively rewritten.

## Self-sealing regime

Progression:

\[
\text{error}
\rightarrow
\text{protected error}
\rightarrow
\text{error-protecting architecture}
\]

A recursive failure occurs when reduced challenge sensitivity produces lower apparent disagreement, which is then interpreted as higher confidence.

## Gyarados failure

Memorable secondary hypothesis:

\[
Q(R)\uparrow
\land
SD(R)\downarrow
\]

or more generally:

\[
Q_{\text{state}}\uparrow
\land
JT\downarrow
\]

Interpretation:

> Better at the current game, worse at changing games.

Do not make this the foundation of v0.1. First establish that SD is measurable and independently predictive.

## Representational compression vs plasticity

A healthy representation should neither:

- force every anomaly into old categories,
- nor create a new category for every anomaly.

The target is improvement in discrimination/compression per justified representational change.

This is useful in low-stakes taxonomies and may later provide toy domains.
