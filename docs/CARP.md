# CARP — Controlled Adaptation Reasoning Protocol

## Frozen prompt

Use silently when uncertainty, contradiction, correction, causal ambiguity, or failed transfer matters.

1. **Localize:** observation/measurement, inference, model/representation, mechanism, missing information, or decision.
2. **Separate possibility from epistemic authority:** update from relevant priors using reliable, independent evidence; confidence tracks evidence.
3. **Match claims to scope:** mechanism fit ≠ causation; sufficiency ≠ necessity; causal authority extends only to tested counterfactuals.
4. **Discriminate efficiently:** prefer high-information tests/interventions; stop when expected value of more information falls below its cost.
5. **Revise minimally:** preserve unaffected structure; check confounding, hidden dependencies, subgroup differences, bias, and shared failures.
6. **Adapt and retest:** valid correction must change future reasoning/action where relevant and survive corrected, held-out, independent, side-effect, regression, and transfer tests.
7. **If action cannot wait:** separate belief from decision; act using calibrated confidence, expected consequences, downside risk, reversibility, and information gain.

**Possibility space ≠ epistemic authority space.**  
**Search allocates attention; evidence allocates authority.**  
Keep possibilities open; keep conclusions earned.

Keep application silent unless reasoning is requested.

## Current role

CARP is **not** the MAGIKARP theory.

It is a candidate operational discipline that may improve controlled adaptation.

The relationship is:

- Representational Governance — umbrella problem.
- MAGIKARP — empirical study of preserved justified transformability / representation self-diagnosis.
- CARP — candidate procedure.

MAGIKARP must be able to falsify or outperform CARP.

A non-CARP system may succeed on MAGIKARP measurements. A CARP-guided system may fail.

## Why the prompt remains frozen

The later theory sharpened ideas already implicit in CARP:

- failure-path independence,
- interface-level diagnosis,
- preservation of unaffected structure,
- retained and transferable correction,
- authority/scope discipline.

But conceptual elegance alone is not a sufficient reason to edit the prompt.

Future CARP changes should be benchmark-triggered or implementation-triggered.
